FAQ

Do you need access to our Shopify or Klaviyo accounts?

No. A standard Shopify order export (CSV) is enough. We never ask for logins.

Will our client know you made it?

No. The report carries your agency's name and branding only.

What happens to the data?

It's used only to build that report and deleted after delivery. Happy to sign your NDA.

Is the sample from a real client?

No. It uses the public "Online Retail II" dataset of a UK gift-ware retailer, so it can be shared openly.

How much does it cost?

The first report is free. After that it's $149 per report, or $399 a month for up to 5 client reports (monthly or quarterly). Most agencies resell it to their client as a premium review.

Which platforms do you support?

Shopify first. WooCommerce and other platforms work too if they can export orders with customer IDs, dates, products and refunds.
